Clear Eyes

Clear Eyes replaces your Home timeline with a cluster view. Each post is judged slop or not by a single rule, then non-slop posts are grouped by topic. Slop is swept into a collapsed sidebar. Tag posts as slop / not slop and the rule is rewritten from your tags.

API

Stored in extension local storage on this machine only, and sent only to api.anthropic.com. Get a key at console.anthropic.com. Consider a dedicated key with a spend limit.
Tuned on claude-sonnet-5. Haiku works but clusters noticeably worse. Any Anthropic model ID works.
Runs each time your tags change (coalesced while one is in flight). Leave blank to use the model above.

Behavior

Media-only posts (no text) are always skipped — there's nothing to cluster.
